Know before you apply

UNDERSTANDING ADMISSIONS

Every country, and every university, works differently. Here's what you need to know before you pick where to apply.

🇺🇸
UNITED STATES

US admissions are holistic — universities look at the whole person, not just grades. A strong story, good essays, and real activities can open doors that grades alone won't. Moroccan students actually have an advantage here — your background is worth talking about.

Annual cost (before aid)
500K – 800K MAD
Top universities like MIT, Harvard, and Yale cover 100% of demonstrated financial need for international students. Your actual cost could be zero.

Scholarship strategy

Focus on need-blind and need-aware schools that offer full financial aid to international students. Schools like Amherst, Dartmouth, Harvard, MIT, Princeton, and Yale commit to meeting 100% of financial need. The right application can bring your cost from 700,000 MAD to zero.

Application components

Essays (Personal Statement)
Critical

Your most important piece. The Common App essay and supplementals tell your story. A Moroccan student who grew up navigating two school systems has something real to say — use it.

GPA / Academic record
Critical

US universities read your full transcript, not just terminale. Your Bac average and school grades all count. We help you present them in a way American admissions offices actually understand.

SAT / ACT
Critical

Most top schools are test-optional, but a strong SAT (1450+) helps a lot for international applicants. It fills in context that your transcript alone can't give.

Extracurriculars
Critical

Activities, leadership, projects, volunteering. Depth over breadth. Starting something beats being a passive member of ten clubs.

Recommendation letters
Important

2-3 letters from teachers and counselors. Getting a real letter in Morocco takes planning. We help you brief your teachers so what they write actually helps you.

TOEFL / Duolingo
Important

Required proof of English. TOEFL 100+ is standard for top schools. The Duolingo English Test is now accepted by most universities and is much cheaper.

🇨🇦
CANADA

Canadian admissions are mainly grades-based — most universities don't ask for essays or extracurriculars. If your academic record is strong, you have a real shot. Tuition is lower than the US, but full scholarships for international students are harder to find.

Annual cost (before aid)
200K – 350K MAD
Lower than the US, but fewer full scholarships for international students. Merit-based awards exist and are competitive.

Scholarship strategy

Look at entrance scholarships at UBC, McGill, and University of Toronto. The Lester B. Pearson Scholarship at UofT offers full funding. Strong grades plus a solid profile are the key.

Application components

Grades / Academic average
Critical

Most Canadian universities admit mainly on your grade average. A strong Bac and consistent school results are your main asset here.

IELTS / TOEFL
Critical

English proficiency is required for all English-language programs. IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+ is standard. French programs in Quebec accept DELF/DALF.

Personal essay (select programs)
Important

Some competitive programs at UBC and McGill ask for short essays. Less weight than the US, but worth doing properly if it's required.

SAT (optional)
Optional

Not required, but a strong score can help for competitive programs like engineering or CS.

Reference letters
Optional

Required for some scholarship applications. Less common for undergraduate admissions than in the US.

Student visa (study permit)
Critical

You'll need a Canadian Study Permit. Apply early — Moroccan students generally get approved, but processing times vary. We walk you through the paperwork.

🇪🇺
EUROPE

Europe is very different from country to country. Germany and the Netherlands focus on grades and subject requirements. France uses grades plus entrance exams. Italy, Spain, and Portugal are much more accessible and affordable. Tuition is often very low or free, but competitive merit scholarships still require a strong profile.

Annual cost (before aid)
0 – 150K MAD
Germany has near-zero tuition at public universities. France, Italy, and Spain are far more affordable than the US. Many programs are taught in English.

Scholarship strategy

Erasmus+, DAAD in Germany, Orange Knowledge in the Netherlands, Campus France. Many European countries have bilateral agreements with Morocco that make scholarships more accessible. Bocconi in Italy and TU Delft in the Netherlands offer merit-based aid.

Application components

Bac / Academic grades
Critical

In most European countries, your Bac results and school average are the main admission criteria. European admissions offices tend to know the Moroccan system well.

Language proficiency
Critical

English programs need IELTS/TOEFL. French programs in France and Belgium accept TCF or DELF. Moroccan students often qualify easily for French programs.

Motivation letter
Important

Most European universities ask for one. Shorter than US essays, but it needs to be specific and clearly written. We help you write one that doesn't sound generic.

CV / Resume
Important

More important than in US admissions. European universities want to see your academic trajectory and any work or research experience laid out clearly.

Entrance exams (France)
Critical

Grandes Écoles like HEC and École Polytechnique have competitive entrance exams. Parcoursup for public universities is grade-based. Students from the French mission are well positioned.

Recognition of Moroccan Bac
Important

Most European countries officially recognise the Moroccan Bac. We help you put your dossier together in the format each country requires.

🇬🇧
UNITED KINGDOM

UK admissions through UCAS are subject-focused and grades-driven. You apply to specific courses, not general programs. Your personal statement has to be about your passion for the subject, not your life story. That's a very different ask from US essays, and it catches a lot of students off guard.

Annual cost (before aid)
280K – 450K MAD
International fees at Russell Group universities are high. Chevening and Commonwealth scholarships are competitive but fully funded.

Scholarship strategy

Chevening Scholarship (fully funded, very competitive), Commonwealth Scholarships, university scholarships at Durham, Edinburgh, and Manchester. Moroccan students with strong Bac results have a solid case.

Application components

Academic grades
Critical

UK universities set specific grade requirements per course. A strong Bac Scientifique or equivalent is essential. They look at predicted and actual grades carefully.

Personal statement (UCAS)
Critical

4,000 characters about why you love your subject — not who you are. This is very different from US essays and trips up a lot of students. We help you make that distinction clearly.

IELTS / TOEFL
Critical

IELTS 6.5-7.5 depending on the program. Russell Group universities usually want higher scores. Duolingo is accepted by some but not all.

Reference letter
Important

One academic reference submitted through UCAS. It should speak specifically to your ability in the subject you're applying for.

Admissions tests
Important

Oxford and Cambridge often require subject-specific tests (MAT for maths, BMAT for medicine, LNAT for law). Start preparing early — these are very different from anything else.

Interview (Oxbridge)
Critical

Oxford and Cambridge interview shortlisted candidates. These are academic discussions, not HR conversations. Preparation is essential and very different from anything you've done before.

🌏
ASIA

Asia's top universities — NUS in Singapore, KAIST in South Korea, HKUST in Hong Kong, Tsinghua and Peking University in China — are increasingly global and offer generous scholarships. NUS is now ranked in the top 10 worldwide. Moroccan students are largely unknown in these markets, which is actually an advantage.

Annual cost (before aid)
60K – 250K MAD
Many Asian governments fully fund international students. South Korea's GKS, Singapore's ASEAN scholarship, and China's CSC scholarship cover everything.

Scholarship strategy

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) — full funding, Korean language training included. Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) — full funding at Tsinghua, Fudan, and others. NUS Merit Scholarships. These pathways are underused by Moroccan students and very much winnable.

Application components

Academic excellence
Critical

Asian top universities are very grade-focused. A strong Bac in Sciences or Maths is your main credential. Top scores matter a lot here.

English / Language proficiency
Critical

English programs need IELTS or TOEFL. Chinese programs may need HSK. Korean programs through GKS include language training as part of the package.

Research / STEM background
Critical

Asian universities lean heavily toward science and engineering applicants. Maths and science Bac students are particularly competitive for NUS, KAIST, and top Chinese schools.

Motivation letter / SOP
Important

A Statement of Purpose explaining your academic goals and why this specific university. More formal than US essays — focused on where you want to go academically and professionally.

Recommendation letters
Important

2-3 academic references. They should speak to your intellectual ability and academic potential, especially for scholarship applications.

Government scholarship application
Critical

This is separate from the university application. GKS and CSC have their own processes and timelines. We handle the full process with you — most consultants don't even know this pathway exists.

🌍
MIDDLE EAST

The UAE and Qatar are home to world-class American and European university campuses: NYU Abu Dhabi, NYU Dubai, Sorbonne Abu Dhabi, Carnegie Mellon Qatar. You get the same degree as the home campus. Moroccan students are well-positioned to compete, and our team includes someone who studied at NYUAD on a full scholarship.

Annual cost (before aid)
150K – 500K MAD
NYUAD meets 100% of demonstrated financial need. Many regional campuses offer substantial merit scholarships. Some are need-blind for all students.

Scholarship strategy

NYU Abu Dhabi meets full financial need for every admitted student. KAUST in Saudi Arabia fully funds all graduate students. AUS, AUB, and AUC offer merit scholarships. This is our strongest pathway — we know NYUAD from the inside.

Application components

Essays (personal + supplemental)
Critical

American campuses in the region use the same holistic process as their US counterparts. Essays matter a lot. Our team has insider knowledge of what NYUAD actually looks for.

Academic record
Critical

Strong Bac results and consistent school grades. NYUAD gets thousands of applications — your record needs to stand out. We help you present Moroccan grades in a way that makes sense to them.

SAT / ACT
Critical

American campuses in the Middle East follow US testing standards. A strong SAT (1450+) makes a real difference, especially for STEM programs.

English proficiency
Critical

TOEFL 100+ or IELTS 7.0+ for NYUAD. Everything is taught in English. Moroccan students from the French mission with strong English are competitive.

Extracurriculars
Critical

NYUAD accepts under 4% of applicants. Depth of engagement in activities, leadership, or research is not optional — it's essential.

Financial aid application
Critical

NYUAD's aid is need-based — you submit a CSS Profile and supporting documents. Our team went through this exact process and guides you step by step.
