Know before you apply

UNDERSTANDING ADMISSIONS

Every country — and every university — has a different philosophy. Here's what you need to know before choosing where to apply.

🇺🇸
UNITED STATES

US admissions are holistic — meaning universities look at the whole person, not just grades. A perfect GPA alone won't get you in. But a compelling story, strong essays, and genuine activities can open doors that grades alone never could. This is where Moroccan students have a real advantage — your background is a story worth telling.

Annual cost (before aid)
500K – 800K MAD
Top universities like MIT, Harvard, Yale meet 100% of demonstrated financial need for international students. Your real cost could be 0.

Scholarship strategy

Focus on need-blind or need-aware schools that offer full financial aid to internationals. Schools like Amherst, Dartmouth, Harvard, MIT, Princeton, Yale, and others commit to meeting 100% of need. With the right application, your cost can go from 700,000 MAD to 0.

Application components

Essays (Personal Statement)
Critical

Your most powerful tool. The Common App essay (650 words) and supplemental essays tell your story. A Moroccan student who grew up navigating two school systems has a unique perspective — use it.

GPA / Academic record
Critical

Strong grades across all years, not just terminale. US universities look at your full transcript. Your Bac average and school grades matter — we help you present them correctly.

SAT / ACT
Critical

Most top schools are now test-optional, but a strong SAT score (1450+) significantly boosts international applicants. It compensates for unfamiliar transcripts.

Extracurriculars
Critical

Activities, leadership, projects, volunteering. Quality over quantity. Being the founder of something beats being a passive member of 10 clubs.

Recommendation letters
Important

2-3 letters from teachers and counselors. In Morocco, getting a meaningful letter (not a generic one) takes planning. We help you brief your teachers properly.

TOEFL / Duolingo
Important

Required proof of English proficiency. TOEFL 100+ is standard for top schools. Duolingo English Test is now accepted by most universities and is much cheaper.

🇨🇦
CANADA

Canadian admissions are primarily grades-based — no essays, no extracurriculars required at most universities. If your academic record is strong, you have a real shot. Some top programs (like UBC or McGill) are highly competitive and do require more. Tuition is lower than the US, but scholarship options for internationals are more limited.

Annual cost (before aid)
200K – 350K MAD
Lower than the US, but fewer full scholarships for international students. Merit-based awards are available and competitive.

Scholarship strategy

Look for entrance scholarships at UBC, McGill, University of Toronto. Programs like Lester B. Pearson (UofT) offer full funding. Strong academic records + a compelling profile are key.

Application components

Grades / Academic average
Critical

Most Canadian universities admit based primarily on your grade average. A strong Bac performance and consistent school results are your main asset here.

IELTS / TOEFL
Critical

English proficiency is required for all English-language programs. IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+ is standard. French-language programs in Quebec accept DELF/DALF.

Personal essay (select programs)
Important

Some competitive programs at UBC and McGill ask for short essays or a personal profile. Less emphasis than the US, but worth doing well.

SAT (optional)
Optional

Not required by Canadian universities, but a strong score can support applications to competitive programs like engineering or CS.

Reference letters
Optional

Required for some scholarship applications and graduate programs. Less common for undergraduate admissions.

Student visa (study permit)
Critical

You'll need a Canadian Study Permit. Apply early — Moroccan students typically get approved, but processing times vary. We guide you through the paperwork.

🇪🇺
EUROPE

Europe is incredibly diverse in its admissions. The Netherlands and Germany focus on grades and specific subject requirements. France (Grandes Écoles) is grade + entrance exam. Italy, Spain and Portugal are very affordable with accessible admissions for Moroccan students. The key: tuition is often very low or even free, but merit scholarships are competitive.

Annual cost (before aid)
0 – 150K MAD
Germany has near-zero tuition at public universities. Netherlands, France, Italy and Spain are far more affordable than the US. Many programs taught in English.

Scholarship strategy

Erasmus+, DAAD (Germany), Orange Knowledge (Netherlands), Campus France. Many European countries have bilateral agreements with Morocco making scholarships more accessible. Italy's Bocconi and the Netherlands' TU Delft offer merit-based aid.

Application components

Bac / Academic grades
Critical

In most European countries, your Bac results and school average are the primary admission criteria. Mention Bac scores clearly — European admissions offices understand the Moroccan system well.

Language proficiency
Critical

English programs need IELTS/TOEFL. French programs in France/Belgium accept TCF or DELF. German programs may need TestDaF. Moroccan students often qualify easily for French programs.

Motivation letter
Important

Most European universities ask for a motivation letter. Shorter than US essays, but it needs to be specific and well-written. We help you craft one that stands out.

CV / Resume
Important

More important than in US admissions. European universities want to see your academic trajectory, activities and any work or research experience clearly laid out.

Entrance exams (France)
Critical

Grandes Écoles (HEC, École Polytechnique) have competitive entrance exams. Parcoursup for public universities is grade-based. Moroccan students with French mission background are well positioned.

Recognition of Moroccan Bac
Important

Most European countries officially recognise the Moroccan Bac. We help you present your dossier in the format each country requires.

🇬🇧
UNITED KINGDOM

UK admissions through UCAS are subject-focused and grades-driven. You apply to specific courses, not general programs. The personal statement (4,000 characters) must be about your passion for your chosen subject — not your life story. Strong A-Level equivalents or a top Bac Scientifique are essential. Tuition is high but some scholarships exist.

Annual cost (before aid)
280K – 450K MAD
International fees at Russell Group universities (Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, UCL) are high. Chevening and Commonwealth scholarships are competitive but fully funded.

Scholarship strategy

Chevening Scholarship (fully funded, highly competitive), Commonwealth Scholarships, university-specific scholarships at Durham, Edinburgh, Manchester. Moroccan students with excellent Bac results have a strong case.

Application components

Academic grades
Critical

UK universities set specific grade requirements per course. A strong Bac Scientifique or equivalent is essential. They look at predicted and actual grades carefully.

Personal statement (UCAS)
Critical

4,000 characters about why you love your subject. Not about who you are — about why you want to study this. Very different from US essays. We help you make this distinction.

IELTS / TOEFL
Critical

IELTS 6.5–7.5 required depending on program. Russell Group universities tend to require higher scores. Duolingo is accepted by some but not all UK universities.

Reference letter
Important

One academic reference submitted through UCAS. Should speak specifically to your academic ability in the subject you're applying for.

Admissions tests
Important

Oxford and Cambridge often require subject-specific tests (MAT for maths, BMAT for medicine, LNAT for law). Prepare early — these tests are highly specialised.

Interview (Oxbridge)
Critical

Oxford and Cambridge interview shortlisted candidates. These are academic discussions, not HR interviews. Preparation is essential and very different from anything you've done before.

🌏
ASIA

Asia's top universities — NUS (Singapore), KAIST (South Korea), HKUST (Hong Kong), Tsinghua and Peking University (China) — are increasingly global and offer generous scholarships. Singapore's NUS is now ranked among the world's top 10. Many programs are taught entirely in English. Moroccan students are largely unknown in these markets — which is actually an advantage.

Annual cost (before aid)
60K – 250K MAD
Many Asian governments fully fund international students to attract global talent. South Korea's GKS, Singapore's ASEAN scholarship, and China's CSC scholarship cover everything.

Scholarship strategy

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) — full funding, Korean language included. Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) — full funding at Tsinghua, Fudan, Zhejiang. NUS Merit Scholarships. These are underused by Moroccan students and highly winnable.

Application components

Academic excellence
Critical

Asian top universities are extremely grade-focused. A strong Bac result (especially Sciences or Maths) is your primary credential. Top scores matter significantly here.

English / Language proficiency
Critical

English programs require IELTS or TOEFL. Chinese programs may require HSK. Korean programs through GKS provide language training. Moroccan students with French background adapt quickly.

Research / STEM background
Critical

Asian universities heavily favour science, engineering and technology applicants. Math and science Bac students are particularly competitive for programs at NUS, KAIST, and Chinese top schools.

Motivation letter / SOP
Important

Statement of Purpose explaining your academic goals and why this specific university. More formal than US essays — focused on academic and career trajectory.

Recommendation letters
Important

2-3 academic references. Should speak to your intellectual ability and academic potential. Important for scholarship applications especially.

Government scholarship application
Critical

Separate from university application. GKS and CSC have their own processes, timelines and requirements. We navigate the full process with you — most consultants don't know this pathway at all.

🌍
MIDDLE EAST

The Middle East — particularly the UAE and Qatar — hosts world-class American and European university campuses offering the same degrees as their home campuses, often with generous scholarships. NYU Abu Dhabi, NYU Dubai, Sorbonne Abu Dhabi, Paris-Sorbonne Qatar, Carnegie Mellon Qatar — these are fully accredited global institutions right in the region, and Moroccan students are well-positioned to compete.

Annual cost (before aid)
150K – 500K MAD
NYUAD meets 100% of demonstrated financial need. Many regional campuses offer substantial merit scholarships. Some are need-blind for all students.

Scholarship strategy

NYU Abu Dhabi is one of the most generous schools in the world — full need met for all admitted students. KAUST (Saudi Arabia) fully funds all graduate students. AUS, AUB and AUC offer merit scholarships. Our team includes an NYU graduate on a full scholarship — this is our strongest pathway.

Application components

Essays (personal + supplemental)
Critical

American campuses in the region (NYUAD, NYU Dubai) use the same holistic process as their US counterparts — essays matter enormously. Our team has insider knowledge of what NYUAD looks for.

Academic record
Critical

Strong Bac results + consistent school grades. NYUAD and similar schools receive thousands of applicants — your academic record needs to stand out. We help you present Moroccan grades in context.

SAT / ACT
Critical

American campuses in the Middle East follow US testing standards. A strong SAT (1450+) significantly strengthens your application, especially for engineering and science programs.

English proficiency
Critical

TOEFL 100+ or IELTS 7.0+ for NYUAD. All instruction is in English. Moroccan students who studied in mission française or have strong English backgrounds are competitive.

Extracurriculars
Critical

NYUAD is one of the most selective schools in the world (acceptance rate under 4%). Depth of engagement in activities, leadership, or research is essential.

Financial aid application
Critical

NYUAD's financial aid is need-based — submit CSS Profile and all required documents carefully. Our team went through this exact process and guides you step by step.
