What we do

Everything should start with fit: academic fit, financial fit, and deadline fit.

We do not push one country or one package. We look at your situation first, then we help you focus on what matters most.

This is why financial fit comes before prestige.

Different countries, different rules.

A US application is not a French application. Canada is not the Netherlands. The UK is not Italy. We help you stop treating all applications the same.

United States

Holistic applications, essays, activities, financial aid forms, and careful school selection.

Europe

Affordable options, motivation letters, grade-based admissions, country-specific platforms, and language requirements.

Canada

Strong focus on grades, program choice, English or French requirements, and more limited full funding.

Middle East and Asia

Selective campuses, scholarship routes, English-taught programs, and options that many Moroccan students overlook.

Smart tools, human judgment.

We can use guided questions and AI-supported reflection to help students organize their ideas, but the application must still sound like the student.

AI should not write fake essays for students. That is not the point.

The useful part is helping students remember details, explain their goals, compare countries, organize documents, and prepare better drafts for human review.

Used properly, it makes the process clearer. Used badly, it makes the application sound generic. We avoid generic.

Questions

What students and parents usually ask.

Do you guarantee scholarships?

No. No serious advisor can guarantee admission or funding. We help you apply more strategically and avoid weak choices.

Do I need a perfect Bac score?

No. Strong grades help, but the right plan depends on your whole profile, budget, countries, tests, essays, and deadlines.

Can my parents join?

Yes. Parents are welcome, especially when discussing budget, documents, and realistic options.

Do you work only with US applications?

No. We can look at the US, Canada, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, depending on what fits the student.

What should I send first?

Send your Bac track, grades, target countries, yearly budget, languages, tests taken, and when you want to apply.
